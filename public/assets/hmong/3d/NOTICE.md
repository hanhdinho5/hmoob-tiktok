# Chibi prototype GLB

The six `*-club-v2.glb` models and nine animation clips per model were generated for this repository by `scripts/club-model-design.mjs` and `scripts/build-chibi-models.mjs`. The four older GLB prototype files are retained for comparison. These are real 3D skinned geometry and follow the repository's declared MIT license. The club models use the separately generated textile image described in `docs/CLUB_ASSET_PROMPTS.md`.

Cultural review is pending. Clothing, accessories, geometric trim and movements are prototypes and must not be represented as verified traditional Hmong attire or dance. No third-party character model or motion-capture asset has been incorporated.
